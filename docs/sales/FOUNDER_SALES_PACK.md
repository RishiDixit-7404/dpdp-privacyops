## Objection Handling

**"We already have a privacy policy."**
Good. This checks whether the product and operational data flows match what the policy assumes.

**"We do not want to share production data."**
Correct. We do not need production data. We work from metadata, masked examples, and local scanner output.

**"Is this a compliance certificate?"**
No. This is technical readiness evidence, not legal certification.

**"Can our engineer run it locally?"**
Yes. The scanner is designed to run inside your environment and only share metadata and masked examples.

**"Why pay for a scan instead of doing it later?"**
Because the hard part is finding the hidden places: logs, support tickets, AI prompts, exports, and workflows. The scan gives the team a short, concrete fix list.
